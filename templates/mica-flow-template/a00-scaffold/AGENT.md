## Seats

Most cycles need one agent. Design-type cycles get two seats:
1. **Builder** — drafts the plan/implementation per the brief.
2. **Adversary** — reads the draft, writes `CRITIQUE.md` to `_run/`: gaps,
   edge cases, unasked questions. The builder dispositions every point
   (fixed / rejected + why) before the cycle closes.

The brief says whether the adversary seat is needed.
